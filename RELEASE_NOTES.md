A guided educational cloud-forest demo: notice hidden life, investigate shelter, and follow a butterfly from egg to adult.

- Seven tutorial chapters with clear goals and highlighted encounters.
- A saved shelter comparison and butterfly life history.
- Five sensory views, four regions, and optional discoveries after the tutorial.
- Existing desktop saves are preserved. Use **? → Begin a fresh tutorial → Save backup & begin** for the new opening.

### Mac installation
Download `Underleaf-macOS.zip`, unzip it, and open Underleaf.app. The app includes Apple Silicon and Intel executables; runtime testing has been on Apple Silicon. This build is locally signed, **not Apple-notarized**, so macOS may block downloaded copies. Use the browser demo for installation-free play.

The downloadable app does not update itself. Download a newer release to replace it; local saves live outside the app bundle. Browser and desktop saves are separate.

Habitat thresholds, sensory discovery cues, and development times are illustrative. Scientific review and novice playtesting remain ongoing. Sources and uncertainty notes are included in the notebook.
